Where do I find my IP address?

Windows: Settings → Network & Internet → Wi-Fi → click your network → look for "IPv4 address".

macOS: System Settings → Wi-Fi → Details, next to your network → "IP Address".

Linux: run ip addr in a terminal and look for the address under your active network interface.
